# Deep Research & Analysis Skill

## Purpose

Exhaustive research workflow for gathering evidence, comparing architectural trade-offs,
and producing well-cited, objective analysis.

## Methodology

1. **State the Research Question**:
   - Formulate clear, unambiguous hypotheses or comparison criteria.
   - Define the scope, constraints, and success criteria for the investigation.

2. **Gather Evidence**:
   - Consult official documentation, specifications, standards, and empirical benchmarks.
   - Note conflicting opinions and contrasting perspectives in the industry.

3. **Synthesize Findings**:
   - Compare alternatives side by side using structured trade-off matrices.
   - Explicitly highlight limitations, risks, and unresolved open questions.

## Invariant

- Research analysis is non-destructive by default and does not mutate project source code.
